Frequently Asked Questions
How do I book an appointment?

Go to the Appointments tab and click "Book Appointment". Select your physician, date, time slot, and reason for visit.

How do I cancel an appointment?

In the Appointments tab, find your upcoming appointment and click Cancel. Cancellations within 24 hours may be subject to a fee.

How do I update my insurance information?

Go to Profile & Insurance tab and use the "Update Care Team" option, or contact your clinic directly.

How do I view my billing statement?

Go to the Billing tab. You can view outstanding balances, payment history, and download your full billing statement.

HIPAA Privacy Notice Summary

Audit Trail Health is required by federal law (HIPAA) to maintain the privacy of your health information. We collect and use your health information to provide treatment, coordinate care, and process billing. Your information may be shared with other healthcare providers involved in your care, insurance companies for payment purposes, and as required by law.

You have the right to: inspect and copy your health records, request corrections, receive an accounting of disclosures, request restrictions on how your information is used, and receive communications about your care privately.

Your records are stored securely. We will never sell your personal health information. For questions, contact your clinic's Privacy Officer.
